About

Who I am. I am a PhD student in Computer Science at AUT and a Master’s graduate from VNU-UET, working at the intersection of medical imaging, biomedical signal processing, and trustworthy AI for healthcare.

What I do. My PhD focuses on retinal imaging for cardiovascular and cerebrovascular risk prediction. Earlier work explored sleep health (OSA detection, smart snoring systems) and data-driven methods for agriculture and nutrition.

What I value. I believe that “By learning to create technology, girls learn to speak up.” Through my PhD at AUT, I aim to build algorithms that serve communities, lower barriers to access, and empower more underrepresented voices in tech.

Research

Current Research

Retinal imaging for cardiovascular and cerebrovascular risk prediction. My PhD research explores how AI applied to fundus images can support early risk stratification for heart and brain health. The goal is to develop methods that are clinically useful, accessible, and trustworthy enough to matter in real screening settings.
